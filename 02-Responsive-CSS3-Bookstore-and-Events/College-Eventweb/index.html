About the Event

TechFest 2026 is the annual technical and cultural festival organized by AYULUMEN University. The fest includes coding competitions, hackathons, technical quizzes, cultural performances, photography contests, and workshops that encourage creativity, innovation, and teamwork.

Highlights

  • National Level Coding Contest
  • 24-Hour Hackathon
  • Technical Workshops
  • Cultural Night

Why Participate?

  1. Improve technical skills.
  2. Win exciting prizes.
  3. Network with experts.
  4. Earn participation certificates.
